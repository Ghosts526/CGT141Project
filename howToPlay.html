Objectives

  • Main Goal: Survive as long as possible while defeating waves of enemy ships.
  • Challenge: Each wave becomes progressively harder, introducing tougher enemies, higher enemy count, and faster spawn rates.

Controls

Keyboard / Mouse:

  • W - Moves your ship up
  • S - Moves your ship down
  • Space - Fires your primary weapon (Blaster)
  • E - Fires your secondary weapon (Missile)
  • Left Click - Interact with buttons
  • ESC - Open and close the pause menu within the game

Touch Screen:

  • Up Arrow - Moves your ship up
  • Down Arrow - Moves your ship down
  • Fire Button - Fires your primary weapon (Blaster)
  • Missile Button - Fires your secondary weapon (Missile)
  • On Screen Touch - Interact with buttons
  • Setting Button - Open and close the pause menu within the game

Core Gameplay

  • Shoot down enemy spaceship by dodging incoming fire

Game Mechanics

Combat:

  • Primary Weapon: Blaster Cannon
  • Secondary Weapon: Missile Launcher
  • Enemy Weapon: Blaster Cannon

Movement:

  • Player: Moves up and down
  • Enemy: Moves toward the player in a stright line

Progression

After each run, you recieve a certain amount of credits based on how many ships you destory.

You can use credits to upgrade your ship to survive longer in each playthrough.

Upgradeable Stats:

  • Increase Health by 1 Point
  • Decrease Fire Delay by 0.05 Sec
  • Decrease Missile Cooldown by 0.3 Sec
  • Increase Shield Health by 1 Point
  • Decrease Shield Cooldown by 1 Sec

Max Stats:

  • Health (LV 26) => 30 HP
  • Fire Delay (LV 25) => 0.2 Sec
  • Missile Cooldown (LV 28) => 2.5 Sec
  • Shield Health (LV 21) => 10 HP
  • Shield Cooldown (LV 28) => 9.96 Sec

Health and Shield

Health:

  • HP is determine by the level of your health stat.
  • Each damage you take decreases your HP by 1 point.
  • When your HP reaches 0 it is game over.

Shields:

  • Shield is determine by the level of your shield stat.
  • Each damage you take decreases your shield by 1 point.
  • Shields takes in all damage recieved, which then transfers it to your HP.
  • When shield reaches 0, it starts a cooldown phase.
  • During the cooldown phase, any damage you recieve will restart the cooldown process.
  • Once the cooldown phase is complete, all your shields will be replenish back up.

Tips for New Players

  • Don't shoot missile too close to yourself as it causes an explosion radius.

HUD Explanation

Health and Shield Bar:

  • Displays current HP and shields.
  • Left is 0% and right is 100%

Saving

Saving is done automatically by the system. But, exiting the game before the Game Over screen pops up, will cause you to lose all of your in-game progress.
